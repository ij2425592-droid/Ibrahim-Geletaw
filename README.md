### 🚀 Handcrafted Projects With Purpose

Here are a few projects where design, engineering, and utility meet:

#### 1. [Ethio-Tourism](https://github.com/ij2425592-droid/Ethio-tourism) — *A Cultural & Interactive Travel Companion*
> An immersive tourism web app dedicated to showcasing Ethiopia's rich heritage, historic routes, and natural wonders.
- **Key Features**: Smart AI Trip Planner, gamified passport stamp collector, 360° virtual views, and dynamic multi-currency converter (ETB / USD).
- **Stack**: `React` • `Vite` • `Modern CSS` • `Vercel`
- 🔗 **[Explore Live Demo →](https://ethiotourisms.vercel.app)**

#### 2. [Wereda 07 Civic Portal & API](https://github.com/ij2425592-droid/wereda-07) — *Modernizing Public Administration*
> Bridging local administration and citizens through a transparent, accessible online portal.
- **Key Features**: Public service directories, civic announcements, and citizen inquiry routing powered by a robust headless CMS.
- **Stack**: `React` • `TypeScript` • `Strapi Headless CMS` • `RESTful API`
- 🔗 **[View Civic Portal →](https://wereda-07.vercel.app)** • **[Explore Strapi API Repo →](https://github.com/ij2425592-droid/woreda-strapi-api)**

#### 3. [EthioCare](https://github.com/ij2425592-droid/ethiocare) — *Community Healthcare Finder*
> Helping people quickly find clinics, doctors, and emergency health services across Ethiopian cities.
- **Key Features**: Clean filtering by specialty, direct contact actions, and location-aware recommendations.
- **Stack**: `React` • `JavaScript` • `Responsive UX`
- 🔗 **[Find Healthcare Services →](https://ethiocare-uq2e.vercel.app)**

#### 4. [Nelliys Storefront & Admin Dashboard](https://github.com/ij2425592-droid/nelliys) — *End-to-End E-Commerce Suite*
> A complete commercial setup connecting an engaging shopping experience with a private inventory & order dashboard.
- **Stack**: `JavaScript` • `CSS Design System` • `Vercel`
- 🔗 **[Visit Storefront →](https://nelliys.vercel.app)** • **[Open Admin Dashboard →](https://nelliys-mgmt.vercel.app)**
